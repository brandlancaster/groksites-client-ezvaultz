The facility

Low-key storage. Controlled access. Private viewing.

EZ Vaults is designed to store crates in a nondescript, secure facility — not a high-visibility self-storage site. There is no big signage. Security comes from controlled access, monitoring, and logged handling.

A facility meant to stay in the background

The first location is planned for the Kearny Mesa / central San Diego area. The street address is withheld on this site and is shared with customers at booking.

Nondescript, no big signage

The building is intended to look ordinary from the street. The service does not rely on a large, marked storage facade.

Controlled access

Entry is designed to be limited to authorized staff and scheduled customers. It is not a walk-in storage hallway.

Monitoring

The facility is designed to be monitored. Specific camera, alarm, and staffing arrangements will be confirmed before launch.

Private viewing room

When you need to get into your crate without a full deploy, you can schedule a private viewing. Appointments only.

Crates logged in and out

Intake, storage, viewing, and release are designed to be logged so each crate can be accounted for while it is in our care.

Address shared at booking

The facility address is not published here. Customers receive it when a reservation or viewing is confirmed.

Facility photographs are not used on this sample site. Layout, security systems, and operating hours for viewings will be confirmed before launch.
