FAQ

Common questions.

Straightforward answers about security, storage, scheduling, and billing. Where a policy is still being written, we say so.

Frequently asked questions

How does security work?

EZ Vaultz is designed around three parts: you lock the vault crate and keep the key or combination; we handle vault crates as sealed units and log them in and out of the facility; and they are stored in a controlled-access, monitored, nondescript location. Viewing is by appointment only. Specific facility systems and procedures will be confirmed before launch.

Who has the key?

You do. After you load the vault crate, you lock it and keep the key or combination. We are not designed to open it during ordinary storage, transport, or handling. If a legal or emergency exception ever applies, that policy will be stated in the customer agreement.

What can I store?

The service is intended for personal or household items that fit in the vault crate, are legal to possess, and are not on the prohibited list. Pack items so they will travel and sit in storage safely. Vault crate dimensions and capacity are still being finalized (TBD), so what fits will be confirmed when specifications are published.

What can’t I store?

A full prohibited-items list will be published before launch. It typically includes hazardous materials, perishable goods, illegal items, firearms and ammunition, and cash. Do not place anything on that list in a vault crate. The published list and the customer agreement will be the final authority.

Are the vault crates waterproof?

The vault crates are designed as rugged, weather-sealed, lockable trunks. This sample does not claim a specific ingress rating or brand specification; those details are still being finalized. Items that are unusually sensitive to moisture should be packed with that in mind.

How do I schedule a private viewing?

Viewings are by appointment at the facility, in a private viewing room. Once scheduling is connected, you will request a time through the booking tool or by contacting us. Walk-in access is not part of the service. The facility address is shared with customers at booking.

How do deploys work?

A deploy is a scheduled trip that returns your vault crate to a location in the service area — typically your original address, or another address we confirm is inside the coverage radius. A per-trip deploy fee applies. After you unload, closeout or a later pickup can be arranged as described at booking.

How does closeout or cancellation work?

Closeout is designed as a final deploy of the vault crate’s contents back to you, after which it is returned to EZ Vaultz and monthly billing ends. Notice periods, final fees, and what happens if a vault crate is not made available for return will be set out in the customer agreement (TBD).

Is my property insured?

Coverage options are being finalized and are not stated as a specific policy or limit on this sample site. We recommend checking whether your homeowner’s or renter’s insurance covers items stored off-site, and what documentation that policy requires. Published coverage details will be provided before launch.

How does payment and billing work?

The pricing model is a monthly storage rate per vault crate plus a per-trip deploy fee. Billing cycle, accepted payment methods, deposits, and when charges start and stop are still being finalized. Sample prices on the pricing page are placeholders; exact rates are set per market.

Where do you serve?

The first location is planned for the Kearny Mesa / central San Diego area, with a defined drive-time radius (approx. XX minutes — final radius TBD). The service may expand later. Contact us, or use the sample ZIP check on the service area page, to ask about coverage.

How long can I store items?

Storage is intended to be month-to-month, with no published maximum term on this sample site. Minimum term, notice to end service, and any long-term terms will be confirmed in the customer agreement (TBD).
