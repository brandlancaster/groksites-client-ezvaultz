What is EZ Vaults

A crate at your door.
Locked by you. Stored by us.

EZ Vaults is a door-to-door storage service. We deliver a rugged, lockable crate, you load and lock it, and we store it in a secure, low-key facility until you want a private viewing or a deploy back to your location.

The crates

EZ Vaults uses rugged, weather-sealed, lockable storage trunks — similar in style to a heavy-duty travel or expedition trunk. Each crate is designed to be packed at your location and locked by you before we move it.

Dimensions, interior capacity, exact materials, and lock type are still being finalized (TBD). This sample does not claim a specific brand or published specification.

You provide and keep the lock’s key or combination. We handle the crate as a sealed unit after you lock it.

Door-to-door process

The service is designed around four steps. Timing windows and scheduling details will be confirmed at booking.

1

We deliver an empty crate

On a scheduled window, we bring an empty crate to your location so you can pack on your own time.

2

You load and lock it

Place your items in the crate and lock it. Do not store prohibited items. You retain the key or combination.

3

We pick it up and store it

We collect the locked crate, log it into the facility, and store it in a secure, low-key location.

4

Viewing or deploy back

Schedule a private viewing at the facility, or a deploy that returns the crate to your location. Closeout is a final deploy of the crate’s contents, after which the crate is returned to us.

Security model

The security approach is designed around customer-controlled locks, logged handling, and a low-profile facility. Some operating details are still being finalized.

Customer-locked crates

You lock the crate and keep the key or combination. In ordinary storage and transport, we handle the crate without opening it.

Sealed and logged handling

Crates are designed to be moved as sealed units. Intake and release at the facility are logged so each crate can be accounted for.

Controlled-access facility

The storage location is designed with controlled access. It is not intended to be a walk-in public storage site.

Monitoring

The facility is designed to be monitored. Specific camera, alarm, and staffing arrangements will be confirmed before launch.

Nondescript location

The facility is intended to stay low-key, without large signage. The street address is shared with customers at booking, not published here.

Viewing by appointment only

Access to your crate at the facility is by scheduled private viewing — not drop-in hours.
