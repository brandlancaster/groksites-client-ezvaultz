How the service works

A simple door-to-door process. You keep the lock. We handle delivery, pickup, and storage.

1

We deliver an empty vault crate

A rugged, weather-sealed, lockable trunk is brought to your location.

2

You load and lock it

Pack what you need stored, then lock the vault crate. You keep the key or combination.

3

We pick it up and store it

We collect the locked vault crate and store it in a secure, low-key facility.

4

View or deploy it back

Schedule a private viewing at the facility, or a deploy back to your location.

What you can expect

Designed as a straightforward, low-profile way to store items you do not need every day — without a traditional self-storage unit.

Waterproof locked vault crates

Rugged, weather-sealed trunks designed to be locked by you. Dimensions and capacity are still being finalized.

Door-to-door service

We bring the empty vault crate to you and pick it up when you are ready. No need to haul belongings to a storage site.

You hold the lock

You lock the vault crate and keep the key or combination. We are not designed to open it in the ordinary course of storage.

Low-key secure facility

Vault crates are stored in a nondescript facility with controlled access and monitoring — not a high-visibility storage lot.

Private viewing

Schedule an appointment to access your vault crate in a private viewing room at the facility.

Deploy back on request

When you need your items, we can return the vault crate to your location. A per-trip deploy fee applies.
